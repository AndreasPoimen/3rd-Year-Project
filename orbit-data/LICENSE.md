## Object classifications and physical dimensions

Data from GCAT (Jonathan C. McDowell): https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/ — CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

gcat-catalogue.json and object-metadata.json are MIDAS extracts: standard numbered objects explicitly in Earth orbit, mapped activity/type codes and physical dimensions joined by JCAT. Source estimate flags are preserved. This metadata is not a source of propagated positions.
