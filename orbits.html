Real data, coverage & attribution

Data: SatNOGS DB / Libre Space Foundation, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The downloadable catalogue snapshot retains original source fields and adds retrieval and licence metadata. This adapted dataset is shared under the same licence.

Coverage is the SatNOGS orbital-elements catalogue, not all satellites or debris in Earth orbit. Only elements within 14 days of the displayed time are plotted. An element set does not guarantee a satellite is operational. Failed or decayed propagation results are excluded.

Positions are SGP4 estimates from real published two-line elements, updated once per second using your device’s UTC clock. They are not live spacecraft telemetry. Amber is the selected object’s next orbit, sampled in the inertial frame and oriented to Earth at the current instant—not a ground track. Markers are enlarged for visibility. This is for education and project context, not collision avoidance or mission operations.

The shared catalogue is refreshed every four hours through GitHub Actions. If the provider fails, the last good snapshot is retained and its age remains visible. GitHub schedules can be delayed or disabled after repository inactivity. Reload this page to pick up the latest snapshot.

satellite.js 6.0.1 and Three.js 0.180.0: MIT licences (satellite.js, Three.js). Coastlines: Natural Earth, public domain. Globe radius: 6,378.137 km. No Orbital Radar data or widgets are used.
